\begin{abstract}
This report compares BEVFusion detections with the output of the DetZero
pipeline on 156 frames from the VF adapted dataset. At a 3D IoU threshold
of 0.50, mAP increases from 16.9240\% to 29.6554\%, an improvement of
12.7314 percentage points. Car and motorcycle improve substantially,
whereas pedestrian performance decreases at this threshold. These results
show improvements in box geometry that center-distance AP alone does not
fully capture. The discussed pipeline uses GRM/PRM without CRM and retains
the detector confidence scores. The reported metrics follow a custom VF
protocol rather than the official Waymo or nuScenes benchmark.
\end{abstract}

\section{Definition of 3D IoU and Average Precision}
The evaluator assumes upright boxes rotated by yaw around the global Z
axis, geometric box centers in global coordinates, and dimensions in
width, length, height order. The intersection area of the two rotated BEV
rectangles is multiplied by their vertical overlap to obtain the
intersection volume:
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{IoU}_{3D}(B_p,B_g)
=\frac{V(B_p\cap B_g)}{V(B_p)+V(B_g)-V(B_p\cap B_g)}.
\end{equation}

Predictions are sorted by descending confidence. Each prediction is
matched to the unmatched GT box of the same class and frame with the
highest IoU. A match is a true positive (TP) when
$\operatorname{IoU}_{3D}\geq\tau$. An unmatched prediction is a false
positive (FP), and an unmatched GT box is a false negative (FN).
Each GT box can be matched only once. The evaluated thresholds are
$\tau\in\{0.25,0.50,0.70\}$.

AP is the area under the interpolated precision envelope over the full
recall interval $[0,1]$. The IoU evaluator applies no confidence cutoff
and does not use the nuScenes minimum recall/precision cutoff of 0.1.
Consequently, TP/FP/FN counts include all candidates remaining after class
and range filtering, rather than only predictions with confidence at
least 0.1. IoU thresholds and confidence thresholds are distinct parameters.

\subsection{IoU 0.25: Modest Improvements}
Car improves by 0.2673 pp, motorcycle by 2.3087 pp, and pedestrian by
0.0744 pp. This relatively permissive overlap threshold already accepts
many baseline predictions. The observed refinement gains are therefore
smaller than those at IoU 0.50.

\subsection{IoU 0.50: Clear Gains for Car and Motorcycle}
Car AP increases from 49.2058\% to 61.7406\%, a gain of 12.5347 pp.
Motorcycle AP increases from 0.5390\% to 27.1301\%, a gain of 26.5910 pp.
These are the strongest improvements in the experiment. Pedestrian AP
decreases from 1.0271\% to 0.0957\%, a change of $-0.9315$ pp, indicating
the need for class-specific investigation.

\subsection{IoU 0.70: High-Overlap Accuracy Remains Limited}
Car AP increases from 2.2945\% to 4.9270\%; motorcycle AP rises to
0.5577\%; pedestrian AP remains unchanged. Overall mAP increases from
0.7649\% to 1.8283\% but remains low. Many refined boxes still fail a
strict geometric-overlap requirement. The aggregate results do not
identify whether the remaining errors primarily involve Z, dimensions,
yaw, or annotation conventions. Inspection of corresponding boxes and
point-cloud crops is required.

\section{Conclusions and Follow-Up Experiments}
\begin{itemize}
\item On this VF adapted dataset, the output after DetZero substantially
improves car and motorcycle 3D IoU AP, especially at IoU 0.50.
\item Pedestrian does not improve consistently: AP decreases at IoU 0.50
and dimension error increases. Its refinement branch should be inspected
separately.
\item Geometric improvements can be measured without CRM. These results
do not demonstrate confidence recalibration.
\item Evaluate tracking-only, tracking+GRM, and tracking+GRM+PRM separately
under the same protocol to isolate module and postprocessing effects.
\item For confidence-threshold experiments, apply the same output filtering
policy to baseline and refined predictions, while distinguishing the
tracking/refinement input threshold from the output confidence threshold.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations and Data Provenance}
This report is generated from the comparison JSON supplied by the user,
not from a new inference run. The accompanying
\texttt{run\_report.json} was not supplied to verify checkpoint identities,
processed/fallback track counts, size policy, Z policy, or the run's input
threshold. Improvements or regressions therefore cannot yet be attributed
to GRM or PRM individually.

The custom 3D IoU metric uses confidence-ordered greedy matching and
full-recall AP. DetZero's original Waymo evaluation uses the Waymo metric
implementation with Hungarian matching. The numbers here must not be
called official Waymo mAP/mAPH. NDS is not reported because the VF
protocol does not evaluate velocity or attributes. GT LiDAR point counts
come from partner metadata and are not recomputed by the evaluator.
Results on 156 frames do not establish generalization to other scenes.
